Performance Strategy

  • Omni-channel ad strategy

    Full-funnel campaigns across Meta, Google, and YouTube working in coordination — awareness through to final conversion — rather than isolated platform-by-platform campaigns.

    Ad creatives were split by audience interest and product category to improve CTR and conversion rate across cold, warm, and retargeting layers simultaneously.

    Repeat visitors were converted into buyers with personalized retargeting messaging — reducing RTO risk and improving revenue per session on warm audiences.
