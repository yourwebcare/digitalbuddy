FAQ

Questions we hear most.

What industries do you have experience in?

We have proven expertise across fashion & apparel, education, real estate, healthcare, and NGOs. With over ₨1 Billion in digital spend managed, we understand industry-specific challenges and build strategies tailored to each sector.

How long does it take to see results?

Performance campaigns can start driving leads and sales within days, while SEO typically takes 3–6 months for noticeable improvements. We create a clear timeline at the start so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you use customized strategies or standard templates?

Every brand is unique, so we never apply cookie-cutter strategies. From funnels to creatives, everything is tailored to your audience, industry, and growth objectives — ensuring you get a personalized plan built to scale.

How do you keep us updated on campaign performance?

We provide transparent reporting dashboards and regular reviews, so you can see exactly where your budget is going and what results it's driving. Our team works as an extension of yours — keeping you informed at every step.

How do you measure success in campaigns?

We focus on ROI-driven metrics like ROAS, cost per lead, customer acquisition cost, and lifetime value — ensuring every campaign contributes directly to your business growth. Not impressions. Not clicks.
